# Responsible disclosure

## Reporting to others

Before contacting a maintainer or bounty programme: confirm the current scope and reporting rules, reproduce the issue with permitted methods, bound its impact and check prior art. Use only the designated private channel. Send the minimum needed to validate the issue, with an exact environment and stated limits. Do not discuss an unresolved issue in public.

When the evidence supports only a low-impact observation, a known issue, a false positive or doubtful eligibility, the research is kept and no speculative report is sent.

## Reporting to Vonkwerk AI

To report a problem in this repository or on vonkwerkai.nl, see [vonkwerkai.nl/security](https://vonkwerkai.nl/security/). This repository contains only toy contracts; it has no deployment and no bounty.
