# Ethics and authorization

Testing is limited to:

- systems owned by Vonkwerk AI;
- intentionally vulnerable labs and CTFs, such as the ones in this repository;
- open-source code in local environments, where the licence and project rules allow it;
- targets covered by an explicit bug-bounty scope or written permission, within that scope.

Scope, exclusions, dates and permitted methods are recorded before work starts. If authorization is unclear, that line of testing stops until the programme's own channel clarifies it.

Not done, under any circumstances: unauthorized access, credential collection, phishing, malware, persistence, denial of service, mass scanning or exploitation, evasion of monitoring, data exfiltration, or moving real assets. Reading public chain data is not permission to change public chain state.

Evidence is kept private, sensitive data is minimised, and unresolved issues are not published in a form that would help someone exploit them.
